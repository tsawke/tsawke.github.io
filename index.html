Oto-Meal

Personalized meal awareness without audio or images

I lead an earable sensing project using photoplethysmography (PPG) and an inertial measurement unit (IMU) to recognize chewing, drinking, coughing, swallowing, talking, and rest. I developed signal-processing and evaluation pipelines, a two-stage neural recognizer, and a lightweight memory matcher evaluated separately for within-user personalization.

RoleProject Lead & Primary Developer
70.99%pooled event accuracy
80.38 ± 0.84%memory matching · 20% calibration
85.13 ± 0.57%memory matching · 60% calibration

Workshop study: window-level event accuracy on a seven-user dataset. Pooled neural recognition and within-user memory matching are evaluated under separate mixed-user and within-user protocols.

  • 7 users, 538 raw recordings, and 7,028 generated windows.
  • Two-stage event/rest gating and five-class behavior recognition.
  • PPG+IMU outperformed either modality alone in the reported memory-matching evaluation.

3D Acoustic Field Reconstruction and Adaptive Acoustic Modeling

3DGS-Acoustic · From acoustic measurements to room geometry

I study sparse acoustic reconstruction from room impulse responses (RIRs), including propagation-based receiver calibration and response prediction at unmeasured locations. Building on DiffRIR reproduction and unified evaluation, my current exploration focuses on room geometry inference (RGI) from multiple acoustic measurements.

Event encoding and inference on edge devices

I develop event encoders for spiking neural networks (SNNs), studying learned subband allocation and reproducible comparisons across wearable and Wi-Fi activity-recognition datasets. ReActLens

Scheduling tool results for language-model agents

I prototype state-aware tool-result delivery and inference resumption, coordinating result ordering with cache residency and bounded cache protection. Recent real-engine experiments compare scheduling policies across complete tool-to-response workflows under concurrent result arrivals.

Current work focuses on measured latency and resource behavior. Manuscript in preparation.
