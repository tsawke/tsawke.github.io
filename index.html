LADS

Lease-Aware Delivery Scheduling of Tool Results for Language Model Agents

Yuxuan Hou and Linshan Jiang · 2026

Submitted to WWW 2027 arXiv submitted

I investigate how concurrent language-model agents share a constrained tool-result return path and reusable inference state. LADS coordinates result delivery with cache protection to reduce the time needed to complete multi-step agent workflows.

My work includes the scheduling system, a real-inference prototype, and controlled and Web-derived workload evaluation.

44.6% lower latencycontrolled bursty tool returns · 5 input seeds
42.4% lower latencyWeb-derived burst replay · 3 input seeds

Mean complete-workflow latency relative to ReAct with first-in, first-out (FIFO) tool-result delivery in the reported burst workloads, using the same model and agent policy.

Submitted to The Web Conference (WWW) 2027.

Oto-Meal: Earable Sensing with PPG and IMU for Personalized Meal Awareness

Personalized meal awareness without audio or images

Yuxuan Hou, Jiao Li, Linshan Jiang, and Jin Zhang.

Earable sensing Workshop paper & ongoing research

First-author paper accepted to and presented at WellComp 2026 (UbiComp/ISWC 2026 Workshop) — Computing for Well-being, October 2026. Forthcoming in UbiComp Companion ’26.

DOI: 10.1145/3798063.3841767 ↗ Preprint · arXiv:2608.16678 ↗

Sep 2025 — Present

I lead an earable sensing project using photoplethysmography (PPG) and an inertial measurement unit (IMU) to recognize chewing, drinking, coughing, swallowing, talking, and rest. I developed signal-processing and evaluation pipelines, a two-stage neural recognizer, and a lightweight memory matcher evaluated separately for within-user personalization.

RoleProject Lead & Primary Developer
70.99%pooled event accuracy
80.38 ± 0.84%memory matching · 20% calibration
85.13 ± 0.57%memory matching · 60% calibration

Workshop study: window-level event accuracy on a seven-user dataset. Pooled neural recognition and within-user memory matching are evaluated under separate mixed-user and within-user protocols.

Follow-up research evaluates transfer from public earable data to self-collected PPG and accelerometer recordings, with held-out-user testing and separate protocols for personalized calibration. A browser-based acquisition and video-review prototype supports preparation for future full-meal studies.

  • 7 users, 538 raw recordings, and 7,028 generated windows.
  • Two-stage event/rest gating and five-class behavior recognition.
  • PPG+IMU outperformed either modality alone in the reported memory-matching evaluation.

Neuromorphic Sensing

Understanding Spike Encoding across IoT Sensing Modalities: An Empirical Study

Yuxuan Hou, Di Yu, Linshan Jiang, and Jin Zhang.

I led the study design, implementation, evaluation, and manuscript writing, with controlled comparisons across six sensing tasks and four modalities: wireless, inertial, electromyographic, and tactile signals. Device evaluation on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin examines recognition accuracy, inference-pipeline latency, and measured power-rail energy.

First-author manuscript submitted to SenSys 2027.

Other exploratory directions: task-aware spike representations, adaptive sensing, and resource-aware edge inference.
